Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Payments for Ocean of Jobs plans are processed by Paddle.com, our online reseller and Merchant of Record. Paddle issues all refunds.

14-day refund

If you're not happy with a paid plan, you can get a full refund of any payment within 14 days of the date it was charged. This covers your first payment and every monthly renewal. You don't need to give a reason.

Free trial

The Basic plan starts with a 7-day free trial. You aren't charged during the trial. Cancel before it ends and you won't be charged at all.

After 14 days

We don't refund a payment more than 14 days after it was charged, or give partial refunds for the rest of a month. You can cancel at any time, and your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

If we stop a paid feature

If we discontinue a feature your plan pays for, we'll refund the unused part of your current period, whenever that happens.

How to ask for a refund

  • Email guyvoloshin@oceanofjobs.com from the address you bought with, or
  • Use the link in your Paddle receipt, or contact Paddle at paddle.net.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Paddle usually processes them within 5 business days, and your bank may take longer to show them. A refund ends the plan it was for.

Your legal rights

This policy doesn't limit any right to cancel or get a refund that you have under the consumer protection law where you live.
