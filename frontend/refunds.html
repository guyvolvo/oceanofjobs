Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Payments for Ocean of Jobs subscriptions are handled by Dodo Payments, our reseller and Merchant of Record. Refunds are processed through Dodo Payments.

14-day refund window

You can request a full refund within 14 days of any payment, including your first payment and monthly renewals. You don't need to provide a reason.

Free trial

The Basic plan includes a 7-day free trial. You won't be charged during the trial. If you cancel before it ends, you won't be charged for the subscription.

After 14 days

Payments are generally non-refundable after the 14-day refund window, and we don't offer partial refunds for unused time in a billing period.

You can cancel at any time. Your subscription will remain active until the end of the period you've already paid for.

This doesn't affect any refund or cancellation rights you may have under applicable law.

If we discontinue a paid feature

If we discontinue a feature included in your paid plan, we'll refund the unused portion of your current billing period.

How to request a refund

You can request a refund in either of these ways:

  • Email guyvoloshin@oceanofjobs.com from the email address associated with your purchase.
  • Use the support link in your Dodo Payments receipt.

Refunds are returned to your original payment method. Your bank or payment provider may take several business days to show the refund.

A refund cancels the subscription associated with that payment.

Your legal rights

This policy doesn't limit any rights you may have under the consumer protection laws that apply where you live, including any statutory rights to cancel a purchase or receive a refund.

See also our Terms of Service and pricing page.
