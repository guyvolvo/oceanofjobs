4. Application limits

Automatic application limits depend on your plan. The planned limits are:

  • Free: 10 applications per calendar month.
  • Basic: 100 applications per calendar month.
  • Pro: Up to 50 applications per day.

Filling out an application without sending it doesn't count toward these limits. Every plan also includes AI answers to new application questions, up to 50 a month on Free, 300 on Basic and 3,000 on Pro.

These limits apply once automatic applications become available for your plan. Check the pricing page for the current availability of this feature.

An application counts when the extension submits it. Applications skipped or stopped before submission don't count. Unused applications don't roll over to the next month.

The daily limit on Pro is intended to prevent excessive application activity. We may change plan limits with at least 30 days' notice by email.

5. Automatic applications

When available, the Ocean of Jobs browser extension can fill in and submit job applications on employers' websites using the profile information, documents, and answers you provide. You can review applications before submitting them or enable automatic submission for jobs you've chosen to apply to.

By enabling submission, you authorize the extension to submit applications in your name according to your settings and selections.

  • Your responsibility: Make sure your profile, resume, and saved answers are accurate and truthful. Applications are submitted in your name, and you're responsible for their content.
  • No guaranteed results: We can't guarantee that an application will be received, reviewed, or answered, or that it will lead to an interview or job offer.
  • Employer websites: Applications are submitted through websites operated by employers and their hiring system providers. Those websites have their own terms and rules. Some employers don't accept automated applications and may reject or block submissions.
  • Your data: Your application profile and saved answers are stored in your Ocean of Jobs account so you can access them from different computers. Your resume file and any AI provider API key are stored in the extension on your device. The resume is sent to the employer you're applying to, while an API key and the information needed to answer application questions may be sent to the AI provider you choose. See our Privacy Policy for more information.
  • AI answers: On Basic and Pro, the extension can ask Ocean of Jobs to draft answers to questions you haven't answered yet. We draft them with Cloudflare Workers AI using your application profile, and save them to your saved answers. AI answers can be wrong; review your saved answers, because you're responsible for what's submitted.
  • Your own AI key: If you add your own API key, the extension may send application questions and relevant profile details to that provider to help draft answers. The provider's terms and usage charges apply to you.
