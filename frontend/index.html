A privacy focused platform for job seekers

Browse over a million jobs without creating an account. Ocean of Jobs follows a local-first approach: data that can be stored on your device stays in your browser by default. If you create an account, you can sync supported data across devices.

We don't sell your personal data. If you sign up for job alerts, we store your email address and alert preferences to deliver them. Resume and work history analysis runs locally in your browser, so your information doesn't need to be uploaded to our servers for analysis.

Features

Salary disclosure

Most companies do not disclose their salary ranges, that's why there is a salary estimation algorithm to help you decide better.

Real-time data

Jobs appear on Ocean of Jobs a few minutes after companies publish them, and at most 2 hours for companies that post less often.

Smart alerts

Sign-up to set up email alerts based on your preferences.

Public API usage

Free JSON API endpoint that does not require an API key.
