Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026.

Ocean of Jobs is an open-source job board. You can browse, search and filter every listing without an account. This page says what the site keeps when you do, and what it keeps once you sign in.

Without an account

The site sets no cookies of its own and runs no advertising. Its pages load Google Analytics, which sets Google's own cookies to count visits and page views.

The site also keeps its own anonymous counts of visits, searches, opened listings and apply clicks, so we can see whether it is growing. Each count carries the event's name and, for a search, the words searched. Nothing else goes with it: no cookie, no identifier, no IP address. Counts are kept as totals per minute. Search words are kept for 90 days, and words that look like an email address or a phone number are dropped before anything is stored.

To offer jobs near you, the site reads the country your connection comes from, as our content network reports it. That country is used for the page you are on and is not stored.

In your browser

Your theme, filters, saved searches, panel layout, starred jobs and a few cached results live in your browser's local storage. So do two flags that stop one visit from being counted twice, and a note that you already answered the location question. None of this is sent to us, with two exceptions once you are signed in. Your sign-in tokens go with each request to show it is you, and your starred jobs are copied to your account so they follow you to other devices.

With an account

You can sign in with Google, GitHub or a one-time code sent to your email. Amazon Cognito keeps your sign-in record, which is your email address and, if you use Google, the name and picture Google provides.

Your account holds your alerts, which are the filters you chose. It holds your profile, which is a list of skills, a seniority, where and how you want to work, and when you want digests. It holds the jobs you starred, and an overview the site computes from your profile. The profile has no name, phone number, employment history or CV in it.

If you use a CV to find your skills, it is read in your browser and never uploaded. Only the skills you keep are saved.

Alert digests go only to an email address you have confirmed. Every digest has a link that turns its alert off.

The contact form

A message sent through the contact form reaches the maintainer by email, with the name and address you give so they can reply. It is not stored anywhere else.

Who processes your data

Cloudflare and Amazon CloudFront carry every request to the site, and our own server on Amazon Web Services answers it. Amazon DynamoDB stores accounts, Amazon Cognito handles sign-in, Amazon SES sends email, and Amazon CloudWatch holds the anonymous counts and search words. If you sign in with Google or GitHub, they handle that step. We do not sell or share your data.

Logs

Cloudflare and CloudFront log requests, including IP addresses, for security and to keep the site running. Our server logs each request's address, time and browser type. It never sees your IP address, because requests reach it through a tunnel, and its log is kept for about a week.

Keeping and deleting

An alert is kept while it is on, and deleting one removes it at once. Delete account on your account page removes your alerts and clears your profile. Your sign-in record, starred jobs and computed overview stay until you ask us to remove them, which we will do in full. You can also ask for a copy of everything we hold about you.

Job listing data

Job postings on this board are scraped from companies' public career pages and public ATS APIs. This is public corporate information, not personal user data.

Changes

If any changes are made to this policy, this page will be updated to reflect them.

Questions

Ocean of Jobs is maintained as an open-source project. For privacy questions or data requests, open an issue on our GitHub repository or write through the contact page.
