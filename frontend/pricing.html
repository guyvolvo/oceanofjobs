Questions

What counts as an application?

One counts when the extension submits it. A job it skips, for example because the form asks something it has no answer for, doesn't count. Unused applications don't carry over to the next month.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. The extension fills in forms from your profile and the answers you save. You can add your own key from an AI provider so it can draft answers to new questions, and the provider bills you for its use.

Where does my CV go?

Only to the employer you're applying to. The CV file stays in the extension on your device. Your application profile and saved answers are kept in your account, so they follow you to any computer.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel any time, and your plan runs to the end of the month you paid for. You can get a full refund within 14 days of any payment.
