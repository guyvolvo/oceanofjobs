The extension isn't available on the Chrome Web Store yet. The instructions below describe how it will work once it's published.

It runs in Chrome and Brave and fills out forms in your browser using your CV and saved answers.

## Install and connect

1. Install Ocean of Jobs from the Chrome Web Store once it's available.
2. Click the extension icon and select **Connect account**. Follow the prompt to connect your Ocean of Jobs account.
3. Add your CV in the extension's settings and your contact details in the **Auto apply** settings on your account page. The extension uses these details when filling out applications.

## Apply from the job board

Click **Auto apply** on a job to add it to your queue.

Every 15 minutes, the extension checks the queue, opens queued jobs in background tabs, fills out their application forms and submits them. It processes up to 20 jobs per queue check, within your plan limits.

Once an application is submitted, the board marks it as **Applied** and shows the application time.

Automatic applications are available for jobs hosted on Greenhouse, Ashby and Workable. The board only shows the **Auto apply** button for supported jobs.

## When the extension needs an answer

Some applications ask questions your CV doesn't cover, such as your notice period or visa requirements.

If you've selected **Draft an answer with AI** in your Auto apply settings, the extension asks AI to draft an answer using your profile. This feature is available on Basic and Pro, or on any plan when using your own AI key.

If the AI can't answer, or you've selected **Skip the job**, the application moves to **Questions you need to answer** in the extension's popup. Questions are grouped by job. Answer them there and the job returns to the queue.

Answers you save to your account can be reused on future applications.

The AI doesn't receive your name, email address, phone number or demographic information such as gender, race or veteran status. You can set demographic answers in your account or leave them blank.

## Fill out applications on other career pages

When you visit a job posting, the extension can offer a lighthouse button in the top-right corner of the page.

Click the button and select **Fill application**. The extension fills in the fields it can match to your saved details. Review the form and click **Submit application** when you're ready.

Some sites require a CAPTCHA or another human verification step. The extension can fill in the form, but you'll need to complete the verification and submit the application yourself.

To show the fill button on every company's career page, enable **Show the fill button on every careers page** in the extension's settings.

Otherwise, the button appears on supported sites, including Greenhouse, Lever and Workday. On other sites, open the extension and select **Fill application**.

If there's no application form on the page, the extension shows **No application detected**. After filling a form, **Review filled fields** takes you to the fields that need your attention.

## Use it with your AI agent

An AI agent connected to the Ocean of Jobs [MCP server](/mcp) can add jobs to your automatic application queue using a personal access token.

The extension processes those jobs the same way as jobs you add yourself. You can create a token from your account page.

## Plans

The free plan includes 30 automatic applications per month. Paid plans include higher application limits and additional AI answers.
