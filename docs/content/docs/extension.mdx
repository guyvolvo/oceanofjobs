It runs in Chrome and Brave and fills out forms in your browser using your resume and saved answers.

## Install and connect

1. Install [Ocean of Jobs from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/kihjppefdadlodaoecffiemijlielhdi).
2. Click the extension icon and select **Connect account**. Follow the prompt to connect your Ocean of Jobs account. If you signed up on another device, select **Log in instead**.
3. Add your resume in the extension's settings and your contact details in the **Auto apply** settings on your account page. The extension uses these details when filling out applications.

## Apply from the job board

Click **Auto apply** on a job to add it to your queue.

Every 15 minutes, the extension checks the queue, opens queued jobs in background tabs, fills out their application forms and submits them. It processes up to 20 jobs per queue check, within your plan limits.

Once an application is submitted, the board marks it as **Applied** and shows the application time.

Automatic applications work on jobs hosted on Greenhouse, Ashby, Workable, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Lever, BambooHR, JazzHR, Recruitee, Teamtailor, Pinpoint, Breezy, Personio and Comeet. The board only shows the **Auto apply** button for these jobs.

To apply without queueing each job, select **Apply automatically** under **Mode** in your Auto apply settings. The extension then applies to jobs that match your preferences, within your plan's limit and any limits you set.

### Sites that check for a person

Some sites check that a person sends the form, with a CAPTCHA or a similar step. On those, the extension fills the application and stops before Submit. The job shows as **Ready to submit** under **Needs attention** on your Auto apply page, and doesn't count toward your plan. Open the application, click **Fill application** in the extension, check the answers and press the site's Submit button.

### Workday

Companies that hire through Workday ask you to create an account on their careers site before you apply. Set a Workday password under **Workday accounts** in the extension's settings, or generate one there. The extension signs in with your profile's email and that password, and creates the account if you don't have one yet. The password stays in your browser.

Some companies email you a link to confirm a new account. Until you click it, that job waits in your history.

### Required boxes

A form can't be sent until its required boxes are ticked, such as agreeing to the company's privacy policy. The extension ticks every required box and answers Yes to required agreements. A required question about marketing emails or a talent pool gets No, since either answer lets the form through. Boxes that aren't required are left as they are.

## When the extension needs an answer

Some applications ask questions your resume doesn't cover, such as your notice period or visa requirements.

With **Draft an answer with AI** selected in your Auto apply settings, which is the default, the extension asks AI to draft an answer using your profile. Each plan includes monthly AI answers: 50 on Free, 300 on Basic and 3,000 on Pro. After that, the extension uses your own AI key if you added one in its settings.

If the AI can't answer, or you've selected **Skip the job**, the application moves to **Questions to answer** in the extension's popup. Questions are grouped by job. Answer them there and the job returns to the queue.

Answers you save to your account can be reused on future applications.

The AI doesn't receive your name, email address, phone number or demographic information such as gender, race or veteran status. You can set demographic answers in your account or leave them blank.

## Fill out applications on other career pages

When you visit a job posting, the extension can offer a lighthouse button in the top-right corner of the page.

Click the button and select **Fill application**. The extension fills in the fields it can match to your saved details. Review the form and click **Submit application** when you're ready.

If the site asks for a CAPTCHA or another human check, complete it and submit the application yourself.

To show the fill button on every company's career page, enable **Show the fill button on every careers page** in the extension's settings.

Otherwise, the button appears on supported sites, including Greenhouse, Lever and Workday. On other sites, open the extension and select **Fill application**.

If there's no application form on the page, the extension shows **No application found**. After filling a form, **Review answers** takes you to the fields that need your attention.

## Use it with your AI agent

An AI agent connected to the Ocean of Jobs [MCP server](/mcp) can add jobs to your automatic application queue using a personal access token.

The extension processes those jobs the same way as jobs you add yourself. You can create a token from your account page.

## Plans

Filling out an application is free on every plan: the extension fills the form and you press Submit. The free plan also includes 10 automatic applications and 50 AI answers per month. Paid plans include more of both.
