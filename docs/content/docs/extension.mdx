The extension runs in Chrome and Brave. It applies in your own browser, with your CV and your answers, so employers see a normal application from you.

## Install and connect

1. Install [Ocean of Jobs from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/kihjppefdadlodaoecffiemijlielhdi).
2. Click the extension's icon, then **Connect account**. Your account page opens and connects the extension.
3. In the extension's **Settings**, add your CV and your contact details. Every application uses them.

## Apply from the board

Press **Auto apply** on a job and it joins your queue. The extension opens each queued job in a background tab, fills in the form and sends it, up to 20 jobs each time it checks the queue. The board then shows **Applied** with the time you applied.

Auto apply works on jobs hosted on Greenhouse, Ashby and Workable. The board only offers the button on those jobs.

## Questions it can't answer

Some forms ask things your CV doesn't cover, like notice period or whether you need a visa. The extension doesn't guess. The job waits in **Questions you need to answer** in the extension's popup, grouped per job. Answer them there or on your account page, and the job goes back in the queue.

Demographic questions (gender, race, veteran status and similar) are never sent to the AI that drafts answers, and neither are your name, email or phone number. You pick those answers yourself in Settings, or leave them unanswered.

## Any other career page

On a job posting outside the board, a lighthouse button appears at the top right of the page. Click **Fill in this page** and the extension fills every field it can match. Check the form, then press **Submit application**.

Some sites protect their form with a CAPTCHA or another human check. The extension fills those forms but leaves sending to you, so press the site's own button.

To show the button on every company's site, turn on **Allow access to all sites** in the extension's settings. Without it the extension only works on the job board's own pages and the sites you click it on.

## Use it with your AI agent

An agent connected through the [MCP server](/mcp) can add jobs to your auto-apply queue with your account token. The extension applies to them the same way as jobs you queued yourself. Create the token on your account page.
