## Cloud storage for the auto apply extension (v1.9.13)

*28 October 2026*

Hello everyone,

I am pleased to announce that the auto-apply extension (v1.9.13) now has cloud storage built and tested to provide you the ability to use settings across different devices.

If you feel that this violates your privacy you have the option to switch back to local storage if you prefer keeping your files and information local. Switching back to local deletes your resume, Workday password, AI keys and all other information from our servers.
