Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Ocean of Jobs is an open-source job board. You can browse, search and filter every listing without an account. This policy explains what data we collect when you use the site anonymously and when you sign in.

Anonymous use

Cookies and analytics. Ocean of Jobs does not set first-party cookies or display advertising. If you accept analytics cookies, pages load Google Analytics, which sets its own cookies to measure visits and page views. If you decline, Google Analytics is never loaded and nothing is sent to Google. Your choice is kept in your browser, and you can change it at any time: .

Usage metrics. We record anonymous counts of visits, searches, listing views and apply clicks to track the site's growth. Each event contains only its type and, for searches, the search terms. No cookies, identifiers or IP addresses are attached. Events are aggregated into per-minute totals. Search terms are retained for 90 days, and terms that resemble an email address or phone number are discarded before storage.

Location. To show relevant local jobs, we use the country your connection originates from, as reported by our content delivery network. It is used only to render the current page and is not stored.

Browser storage

The following data is kept in your browser's local storage and is not sent to our servers: theme, filters, saved searches, panel layout, saved jobs, cached results, flags that prevent duplicate visit counts, and your response to the location prompt.

Once you sign in, two exceptions apply. Your authentication tokens are sent with each request to verify your identity, and your saved jobs are synced to your account so they are available across devices.

Accounts

Sign-in. You can sign in with Google, GitHub or a one-time code sent to your email. Amazon Cognito stores your sign-in record: your email address and, if you use Google, the name and profile picture Google provides.

Account data. Your account stores:

  • Alerts (your saved filter criteria)
  • Your profile: skills, seniority, preferred work location and arrangement, and digest schedule
  • Saved jobs
  • An overview generated from your profile

Your profile does not include your name, phone number, employment history or CV unless you set up automatic applications (see below).

CV parsing. If you use a CV to extract skills, it is processed entirely in your browser and never uploaded. Only the skills you choose to keep are saved.

Email digests. Digests are sent only to verified email addresses. Each digest includes a link to disable the alert.

Personal access tokens

You can create personal access tokens for the MCP server, and connecting the extension creates one. For each token we store its name, its last four characters, when it was created and last used, and a one-way hash of it. The token itself is shown to you once and is not stored. Revoking a token deletes its record.

Browser extension and automatic applications

If you use automatic applications, your account also stores your application profile: contact details, links, work authorization, salary expectations, the answers you save for application questions, and, only if you choose to fill them in, demographic answers such as gender or veteran status. It is kept so the extension can use it on any computer you sign in on, and you can edit or delete it on your account page.

Your CV file and any AI provider key stay in the extension on your device and are never sent to us. When the extension applies to a job, it sends your details and CV only to that employer's application form. If you add an AI provider key, the extension sends application questions and relevant profile details to that provider using your key. We record which jobs you applied to and how many applications you have used, so you can see your history and your plan's monthly limit can be counted. When you send a form with the extension's button on a site it has no dedicated filler for, the history keeps that page's title and address.

On Basic and Pro, if you choose AI answers, a question you haven't answered is sent from our server to Cloudflare Workers AI together with the job's title, company and location and the parts of your application profile needed to answer it. Your name, email address, phone number, CV and demographic answers are never sent. An answer drafted while the extension applies for you is saved to your saved answers, where you can edit or delete it. Suggestions shown on a form you are filling in yourself are not saved.

The extension reads pages only on Ocean of Jobs and on the job and application sites our listings come from, such as Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Comeet, Teamtailor, Workday and SmartRecruiters. Chrome lists each one under the extension's site access in chrome://extensions. On those sites it looks for an application form, or a job posting with an Apply button, in your browser and, if it finds one, shows a small button that fills it in when you click it. It applies on its own only through Greenhouse, Ashby and Workable. On any other site it reads a page only when you open the extension on that tab, to check for an application form in your browser, and fills it only when you click "Fill application".

You can also let the extension see every site, in its settings or when you first use "Fill application" on a site outside that list. With that access it checks each page you open, in your browser, for an application form, so it can show the button on company careers pages and fill forms a page embeds from another site. On a page without an application form it does nothing else, and it sends nothing about the pages you visit to us or anyone else. You can take the access back at any time in the extension's settings or at chrome://extensions.

The extension contains no analytics or tracking and does not collect your browsing history.

Removing the extension deletes your CV and AI provider key from your browser. Deleting your account deletes your application profile, application history and queue, and disconnects the extension.

Payments

Paid plans are sold by Paddle.com, our Merchant of Record. Paddle collects and processes your payment details, billing address and tax information under its own privacy policy, and we never see your card details. Paddle tells us your email address, plan, subscription status and the country used for tax, so we can give your account its plan.

Contact form

Messages submitted through the contact form are delivered to the maintainer by email, along with the name and email address you provide, so we can respond. They are not stored anywhere else.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare and Amazon CloudFront: request delivery
  • Cloudflare Workers AI: AI answers to application questions, on Basic and Pro
  • Amazon Web Services: application hosting
  • Amazon DynamoDB: account storage
  • Amazon Cognito: authentication
  • Amazon SES: email delivery
  • Amazon CloudWatch: anonymous metrics and search terms
  • Google and GitHub: authentication, if you choose to sign in with them
  • Paddle: payments for paid plans

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone beyond the providers listed above.

Logs

Cloudflare and CloudFront log requests, including IP addresses, for security and operational purposes. Our application server logs the requested URL, timestamp and user agent of each request. Because traffic reaches the server through a tunnel, it never receives your IP address. Server logs are retained for approximately one week.

Retention and deletion

Alerts are retained while active and removed immediately when deleted. Delete account removes your alerts, revokes your personal access tokens and clears your profile. Your sign-in record, saved jobs and generated overview are retained until you request their removal, at which point they are deleted in full. You may also request a copy of all data we hold about you.

Job listings

Listings are collected from companies' public career pages and public applicant tracking system APIs. This is publicly available corporate information, not user data.

Why we use your data

We use account data, alerts, saved jobs and plan details to provide the service you signed up for. We use anonymous metrics and logs because we have a legitimate interest in keeping the service secure, working and improving. Paddle processes payment data to complete your purchase and meet its legal obligations.

Our servers and account storage are in Amazon Web Services' Israel region. Israel is recognised by the European Commission as giving adequate protection to personal data. Some providers listed above, such as Cloudflare, Google and Paddle, may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you, in a portable format;
  • correct data that is wrong;
  • delete your data;
  • stop or limit a particular use of it.

Email the address above. We reply within 30 days, and we may ask you to confirm the request from your account's email address. Depending on where you live, you can also complain to your data protection authority: in the EU or UK, the authority in your country; in Israel, the Privacy Protection Authority.

We do not sell your personal information, and we will not treat you differently for using any of these rights.
