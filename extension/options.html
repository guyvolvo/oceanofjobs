Your own AI key

AI answers are included on Basic and Pro, so you only need a key on Free. When a required question has no saved answer and your account is set to use AI, the extension drafts one with this key and saves it to your answers. Basic and Pro also use it once the month's included AI answers run out. Without either, the job is skipped and listed as needing your answer.

Every site

The fill button shows on the job sites Ocean of Jobs lists. With access to every site it also shows on company careers pages, and "Fill in this page" can fill a form a page embeds from another site. Each page is checked in this browser for an application form. Pages without one are left alone, and nothing about the pages you visit is sent anywhere.
