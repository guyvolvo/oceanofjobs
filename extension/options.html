Your own AI key

Every plan includes AI answers each month: 50 on Free, 300 on Basic and 3,000 on Pro. Add your own API key here to keep getting answers after those run out.

In automatic runs, if you chose to have AI draft answers, the extension drafts answers to required questions and adds them to your saved answers. When you fill a page yourself, it suggests answers, outlined in amber, and doesn't save them.

Without an available AI answer or a saved answer, the application will be marked as needing your input.

Fill button

By default, the fill button appears on the hiring sites the extension supports, like Greenhouse, Lever and Workday. Enable access to all sites to use it on company career pages and forms embedded from other websites.

Pages are checked for forms in your browser. When you click Fill or Submit, the page address and job title are sent to Ocean of Jobs. On Basic and Pro, questions without a saved answer are sent too, so AI can draft them.
